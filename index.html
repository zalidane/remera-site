Remera™

Eat smarter. Simply.

Remera™ helps you make smarter food decisions in seconds—no tracking, no logging, no guesswork.

Whether you're ordering at a restaurant or evaluating a meal, Remera gives you clear, personalized insights based on your body and your goals.

How It Works

  • Rate your meal Get an instant score, calorie estimate, and actionable tips to improve your choice.
  • Scan a menu Instantly find the best options based on your goal—lose fat, maintain, or build muscle.
  • Personalized guidance Remera uses your metabolic profile to give recommendations tailored to you—not generic advice.

Why Remera™

  • Know what to order—without overthinking
  • See the best and worst options instantly
  • Get clear, actionable suggestions (not vague advice)

No Tracking. No Logging.

Remera doesn't require food tracking, calorie logging, or tedious data entry. Just make better decisions—moment to moment.

Your Metabolic Profile

Remera uses the Mifflin-St. Jeor equation to estimate your resting metabolic rate (RMR) and uses that to provide meal recommendations tailored to your body and goals.

Learn more about RMR and the Mifflin-St. Jeor equation in the original research publication.
