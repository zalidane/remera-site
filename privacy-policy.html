Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026 · Last updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Remera (“Remera,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Remera mobile application for iOS and Android (the “App”) and the related nutrition analysis service operated at our API endpoint (the “Service”).

Developer / Data Controller: Antonio Delgado
Contact: remera.support@gmail.com
Website: https://zalidane.github.io/remera/

Summary

Remera helps you rate meals and scan restaurant menus using AI-powered nutrition guidance. Key points:

  • No account required. We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address to use the App.
  • Profile data stays on your device unless you use a feature that needs personalization (meal rating or menu analysis).
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Camera images are processed on your device. Only extracted menu text—not photos—is sent to our servers when you analyze a menu.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide in the App (stored on your device)

When you complete onboarding or update your profile, the App stores the following locally on your device:

Data Purpose
Name (optional)Personalization (e.g., greetings)
Date of birth / ageCalorie and nutrition calculations
GenderCalorie and nutrition calculations
Height and weightCalorie and nutrition calculations
Activity levelCalorie and nutrition calculations
Health goal (e.g., lose fat, maintain, gain muscle)Personalized meal and menu guidance
Computed nutrition targets (RMR, daily calorie target)Display and API personalization

This profile data is saved using on-device storage (iOS: UserDefaults; Android: DataStore). We do not maintain a user database or persist your profile on our servers.

You can delete your profile at any time from the Profile screen. Deleting your profile removes locally stored profile data from your device.

1.2 Information sent when you use App features

When you use certain features, the App sends data over the internet to our Service so we can generate results for you:

Feature Data sent Recipient
Meal rating Meal description you enter; relevant fields from your profile (name, age, gender, height, weight, activity level, goal, calorie targets) Our API server, which forwards content to OpenAI for AI analysis
Menu scanning Text extracted from a menu photo via on-device OCR; relevant fields from your profile Our API server, which forwards content to OpenAI for AI analysis

Menu photos are not uploaded. The App uses on-device optical character recognition (Apple Vision on iOS; ML Kit on Android) to extract text from images captured with your camera. Only the extracted text is transmitted for analysis.

We do not use these requests to build a persistent user profile on our servers. Our API is stateless: it processes each request and returns a result without storing your meal descriptions, menu text, or profile in a database.

1.3 Device and usage information (stored on your device)

To enforce free-tier limits and manage premium access, the App stores device-local information:

Data Purpose Storage
Daily meal analysis count Free-tier usage limits (resets daily) iOS: device Keychain; Android: encrypted local preferences
Subscription / premium status Unlock premium features Verified locally via Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android)

This usage data is tied to your device, not to a Remera account. If you delete your profile, your daily usage count may remain on the device by design so free-tier limits cannot be reset by deleting and re-creating a profile.

We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) and do not use cross-app tracking for advertising.

1.4 Information collected automatically by our servers

When the App contacts our API, our hosting provider and server software may automatically receive standard technical data, such as:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of the request
  • Request path and HTTP method
  • User-Agent string (the App sends Remera/1.0)
  • Error and performance logs

In production, our API may temporarily log a short preview of menu text submitted for analysis (for debugging and operations). We do not intentionally log full meal descriptions or profile fields in routine operation, though error logs may occasionally include request-related metadata.

Our API is hosted on Railway. Server access logs are retained only as long as needed for security, troubleshooting, and legal compliance, then deleted or aggregated.

1.5 Information handled by Apple and Google (subscriptions)

If you purchase Remera Premium:

  • Apple (iOS): Payment, subscription management, and purchase verification are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We receive only the information needed to confirm whether you have an active entitlement (e.g., product ID, transaction status, expiration). We do not receive your Apple ID, payment card number, or billing address.
  • Google (Android): When Google Play Billing is enabled, the same applies: Google processes payments; we receive only subscription status needed to unlock features.

Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

We do not use your information for:

  • Selling or renting personal data to third parties
  • Targeted advertising or cross-app profiling
  • Building marketing lists

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and on our servers. Profile and usage data on your device benefit from platform security (including encrypted storage for usage limits on Android and Keychain on iOS).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You use the App at your own risk.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access personal data we process

  • Delete data (use “Delete Profile & Data” in the App; uninstall the App to remove local data)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability (export is not currently built into the App; contact us for assistance)

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You may contact us to exercise your rights.

EEA/UK residents (GDPR): You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern.

To exercise rights regarding data processed on our servers, email remera.support@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request. Because we do not operate user accounts, we may ask for information that helps us locate relevant server logs (e.g., approximate date/time of a request).
